Auli

Discover authentic travel insights, verified seasonal advice, and route guidance for this Himalayan destination.

Overview & Geographical Setting

Auli is India's premier high-altitude ski resort nestled in the Chamoli district of Uttarakhand. Flanked by pristine coniferous and oak forests, Auli offers breathtaking 360-degree panoramic vistas of Himalayan giants including Nanda Devi (India's second highest peak), Kamet, Mana Parvat, and Dunagiri.

Top Attractions & Landmarks

Auli Artificial Lake

One of the world's highest man-made lakes, created to feed snow guns for the ski slopes during early winter.

Joshimath-Auli Ropeway

A 4-kilometer long cable car journey offering sweeping views of the Alaknanda River gorge and snowy ridges.

Gorson Bugyal

An expansive alpine meadow situated 3 km beyond Auli, ideal for beginner day treks amidst alpine flora.

Chattrakund Lake

A tranquil small sweet-water lake hidden within dense oak and pine woods near Gorson Bugyal.

Activities & Experiences

  • Skiing and Snowboarding lessons under certified instructors (Jan - Mar)
  • High-altitude cable car ride over pine valleys and oak forests
  • Scenic high-altitude photography of Nanda Devi and Trishul peaks
  • Day trekking to Gorson Bugyal and Chattrakund alpine lake

Practical Travel Tips & Route Guidance

Traveler's Advisory

Winter temperatures can plunge below -8°C. Carry multi-layered thermal wear, polarized sunglasses, and snow boots. During peak snowfall, vehicles without snow chains may be restricted at Joshimath, requiring travelers to take the cable car up to Auli.

Responsible Mountain Travel

  • Keep the delicate alpine bugyals free of plastics, bottles, and snack wrappers.
  • Hire local certified ski instructors and homestay hosts from Joshimath to support the mountain community.
  • Stay strictly on marked trails to prevent soil erosion on delicate alpine slopes.

Fast Facts

  • Altitude 2,800 to 3,050 meters (9,186 to 10,000 ft)
  • Best Time to Visit Late December to March (Skiing); April to June (Alpine Greenery)
  • Nearest Railway Station Rishikesh Railway Station (approx. 250 km)
  • Nearest Airport Jolly Grant Airport, Dehradun (approx. 270 km)
