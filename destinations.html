Nainital

Cradled in the Kumaon hills around the emerald-tinted Naini Lake, Nainital is famed for serene boat rides, the historic Naina Devi Temple, and panoramic snow views from the cable car ridge.

Best Time: March to June, October to December

Hill Stations • Wildlife & Nature Garhwal
Dehradun District | Altitude: 2,005 m

Mussoorie

The Queen of the Hills offers colonial heritage promenades, tumbling cascades at Kempty Falls, pleasant walks along Camel's Back Road, and panoramic viewpoints overlooking the Doon Valley.

Best Time: April to June, September to November
Explore Mussoorie
Adventure • Spiritual & Pilgrimage Garhwal
Dehradun District | Altitude: 340 m

Rishikesh

The world capital of yoga sits where the sacred Ganga emerges from the Himalayan foothills. Renowned for thrilling white-water rapids, riverside ashrams, and twilight Ganga Aarti.

Best Time: September to November, February to May
Explore Rishikesh
Hill Stations • Adventure • Trekking Chamoli
Chamoli District | Altitude: 2,800 - 3,050 m

Auli

India's premier high-altitude ski resort offers powdery snow slopes, Asia's highest cable car, and unhindered 360-degree panoramas of Nanda Devi and Kamet Himalayan peaks.

Best Time: Late December to March (Snow), April to June (Green)
Explore Auli
Spiritual & Pilgrimage Haridwar
Haridwar District | Altitude: 314 m

Haridwar

The revered 'Gateway to the Gods' where the holy Ganga enters the plains. Experience the soul-stirring evening Ganga Aarti at Har Ki Pauri and historic hilltop shrines via ropeway.

Best Time: October to April
Explore Haridwar
Spiritual & Pilgrimage • Trekking Rudraprayag
Rudraprayag District | Altitude: 3,583 m

Kedarnath

One of the twelve sacred Shiva Jyotirlingas, this ancient stone temple stands majestically beneath the snow-covered Kedarnath peak along the glacial waters of the holy Mandakini river.

Best Time: May to June, September to October
Explore Kedarnath
Adventure • Trekking • Wildlife Rudraprayag
Rudraprayag District | Altitude: 2,680 m

Chopta

Known as the 'Mini Switzerland of India', Chopta is a pristine meadow surrounded by rhododendrons and pines. It serves as the base camp for the Tungnath Shiva Temple and Chandrashila summit.

Best Time: April to June, October to December
Explore Chopta
Trekking • Wildlife & Nature Chamoli
Chamoli District | Altitude: 3,352 - 3,658 m

Valley of Flowers

A UNESCO World Heritage National Park where hundreds of rare alpine wildflowers carpet a glacial river basin in vibrant shades during the monsoon season beneath soaring glaciated peaks.

Best Time: Mid-July to Mid-September
Explore Valley of Flowers

Jim Corbett National Park

India's pioneer national park located in the Shivalik foothills. Famous for open jeep tiger safaris in the Dhikala and Bijrani zones along the scenic Ramganga river.

Best Time: November to May (Dhikala zone opens Nov 15)
